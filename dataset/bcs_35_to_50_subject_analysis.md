# BCS Question Bank Analysis: Standard 200-Question Preliminary Exams (35th to 50th BCS)

> **Filtered Dataset:** Exclusively analyzes standard 200-question preliminary exams between the **35th BCS** and **50th BCS**. Special examinations with less than 200 questions (39th, 42nd, 48th, and 49th Special BCS with 100 questions each) have been **dropped** to ensure an accurate, like-for-like analysis of the full 200-mark syllabus.

---

## Executive Summary

- **Exam Criteria:** Preliminary examinations with exactly **200 questions**.
- **Total Exams Analyzed:** **12 Exams** (35th, 36th, 37th, 38th, 40th, 41st, 43rd, 44th, 45th, 46th, 47th, and 50th BCS)
- **Dropped Exams (<200 questions):** 4 Special Exams
  - **39th BCS** (Special / Doctors — 100 questions)
  - **42nd BCS** (Special / Doctors — 100 questions)
  - **48th BCS** (Special — 100 questions)
  - **49th BCS** (Special — 100 questions)
- **Total Questions Analyzed:** **2,400 questions** (12 exams × 200 questions)
- **Time Period:** March 2015 – January 2026
